Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains how ARCUS Publisher, the internal publishing tool of ARCUS LLC ("ARCUS", "we"), handles information. The general privacy policy of ARCUS LLC is at arcus.consulting/privacy.

1. YouTube API Services

ARCUS Publisher uses YouTube API Services. By using ARCUS Publisher, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy applies to the information Google processes through YouTube.

2. What information the tool accesses

Through the YouTube Data API, with the permissions "upload videos" (youtube.upload) and "view your YouTube account" (youtube.readonly), granted by ARCUS for its own channel only, the tool:

  • uploads videos produced by ARCUS, with their title, description and tags, to ARCUS's own channel;
  • reads the identity of ARCUS's own channel and the public statistics of ARCUS's own videos (views, likes, comments counts).

It does not access, collect or store data about any other YouTube user or channel. It sets no cookies and contains no tracking.

3. How the information is used, stored and shared

  • The OAuth credential (refresh token) is stored only on ARCUS's own computer, in a file readable by ARCUS's operator account, and is used only to call the YouTube Data API for ARCUS's channel.
  • The statistics of ARCUS's own videos are stored on the same computer for ARCUS's internal reporting.
  • No information obtained through YouTube API Services is sold, shared with or disclosed to third parties.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • When access is revoked, or on request to info@arcus.consulting, the stored credential is deleted at once and the stored statistics within 30 days.
  • Statistics are not kept longer than needed for ARCUS's reporting, and in any case no longer than the period allowed by the YouTube API Services policies.
