ARCUS Publisher

ARCUS Publisher is the internal publishing tool of ARCUS LLC, an AI governance and data protection consultancy in Doha, Qatar. It publishes the short educational videos that ARCUS produces itself to ARCUS's own YouTube channel, @arcusgcc, and reads the statistics of that channel.

YouTube This tool uses YouTube API Services.

How it works

  1. ARCUS produces a video and its title and description.
  2. A person at ARCUS approves the video (nothing is uploaded without this approval).
  3. ARCUS Publisher uploads the approved video to the ARCUS channel with the YouTube Data API (videos.insert) and declares it as containing synthetic media where the voice is generated.
  4. It regularly reads the views and engagement figures of ARCUS's own videos (channels.list, videos.list) for ARCUS's internal reporting.

Who can use it

Only ARCUS staff. The tool has no public sign-up, no third-party users, and does not access any other channel or any other person's data.
